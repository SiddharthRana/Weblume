## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Weblume checks a web page for speed, SEO, accessibility and technical problems, then explains what matters and how to fix it in plain English. No jargon, no wall of numbers: just a clear, prioritised list you can act on or hand to your developer.

WHAT YOU GET IN EVERY REPORT
• Lighthouse scores for performance, accessibility, SEO and best practices, on mobile and desktop, measured by Google PageSpeed Insights
• Up to 50 links checked for broken pages and redirects
• Titles, headings, meta descriptions, robots.txt and the sitemap checked
• Every issue ranked by how much it affects your visitors, so you know where to start
• For each issue: what it means, why it matters, and step-by-step instructions to fix it

HONEST BY DESIGN
Every report says what was measured, what was detected automatically and what we recommend. If a data source fails, we tell you instead of guessing. Automated audits are a starting point: they can't guarantee accessibility compliance, search rankings or security.

KEEP AN EYE ON YOUR SITE
• Monitor a site and Weblume re-scans it daily, weekly or monthly
• Choose when to get an email: whenever anything changes, only when new issues appear, only when a score drops, or never
• Get told if your site can't be reached

SHARE AND COMPARE
• Download or share a PDF report (Starter)
• Share a read-only link with a client or colleague (Starter)
• Compare two scans to see what changed (Starter)

ONE ACCOUNT, WEB AND PHONE
Your scans, reports and plan are the same on weblu.me and in the app. Light and dark themes, or match your phone.

PLANS
Start free with 5 scans a month and one monitored site. Starter adds 60 scans per billing period, 12 months of report history, PDF exports, share links, comparisons, code fix suggestions and more monitored sites, checked daily. Subscriptions bought in the app are billed and managed through Google Play.

Only scan websites you own or are allowed to evaluate. Weblume checks one public page at a time, never logs in or submits forms, and follows robots.txt when checking links.
